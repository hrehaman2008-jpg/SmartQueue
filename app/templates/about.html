About SmartQueue

Digital token and queue management for modern clinics.

About This Project

SmartQueue is a digital queue management system for hospitals and clinics, replacing paper tokens with secure, scannable digital tokens.

Built with Python Flask, Flask-SQLAlchemy, Flask-Login, and Flask-WTF, with Bootstrap 5 on the front end.

Security

  • Passwords hashed with Werkzeug's secure password hashing
  • Session authentication via Flask-Login with server-side role enforcement
  • CSRF protection on every state-changing form
  • Token QR codes and status visible only to the owning patient and staff

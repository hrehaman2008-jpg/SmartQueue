How It Works
  1. Register or log in as a patient to get started.
  2. Book an appointment with a doctor, or register as a walk-in at the desk.
  3. Receive your digital token with a scannable QR code.
  4. Check your queue position on your dashboard — refresh for the latest.
  5. Get called & consult — doctors call patients in order and close the visit.

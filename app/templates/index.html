How It Works

  1. 1. Register / Log in as a patient to get started.
  2. 2. Book an appointment with a doctor or register as a walk-in patient.
  3. 3. Receive your digital token with a QR code.
  4. 4. Check your live queue position from your dashboard.
  5. 5. Call & consult — doctors call patients in order and complete consultations.
